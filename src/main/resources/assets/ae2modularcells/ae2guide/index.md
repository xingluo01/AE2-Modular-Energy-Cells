# AE2 Modular Energy Units

An Applied Energistics 2 add-on that turns energy cells into modular blocks: snap modules
(any mix of tiers) together into a solid cube and the whole thing acts as one energy storage on the ME network.

## Contents

* [Modular Energy Unit](items-blocks-machines/energy_cell_module.md): the three tiers, the component
  slots and the energy module terminal.
* [Monitors](items-blocks-machines/monitors.md): the panels that report stored energy and flow.
* [Gain Algorithm](gain-algorithm.md): the two formulas, their parameters, and the numbers when full.
* [Blank Energy Unit](items-blocks-machines/blank_energy_unit.md): the crafting intermediate.
